Remembering His Art

His artistic evolution, from “K.I.D.S.” to “Circles,” reflects not just a career, but a human journey — one defined by growth, resilience, and deep emotional resonance. As my favorite artist, his music has had a profound impact on my life and the lives of millions around the world, leaving a legacy that continues to inspire creativity, healing, and connection across generations.
